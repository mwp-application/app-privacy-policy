Privacy Policy — Liquid Glass

Last updated: 18 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Liquid Glass Android app (“the app”, “we”) handles your information. In short: the app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything it does happens on your device.

Information we collect

None. Liquid Glass has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. We do not collect, store on our servers, or transmit any personal or device information. The app does not require an internet connection to work.

Access to your installed apps

To create a matching glass icon for every app you have, the app reads the list of applications installed on your device. This information is used only on your device, at the moment you generate an icon pack, to produce the icons. It is never uploaded, shared, or sent anywhere, and it is not retained after the pack is created.

The icon pack you create

When you tap “Create my icon pack”, the app builds a personal icon-pack file and installs it on the same device using Android’s standard installer. This file stays on your phone. For your protection against unauthorized redistribution, each generated pack includes a one-way (irreversible) hash derived from your device identifier as an internal watermark. This value stays inside the file on your device and is never transmitted to us or to any third party.

Purchases

Liquid Glass is a paid app sold through Google Play. All payment processing is handled by Google Play; we never see or store your payment details. Please refer to Google’s Privacy Policy for how Google handles purchase information.

Children

The app is a general-audience personalization tool and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new “Last updated” date.
